Verdict

The EA is close to break-even, and this data cannot establish that it is profitable. It returns +$304 on a $10,000 account over 12.4 months under the shipped defaults — but flipping one unresolvable modelling assumption takes that to −$85, the result is not statistically distinguishable from zero, and every dollar of it comes from the first half of the sample.

The model's own probability output does not rank outcomes. Loosening its flagship filter from 0.70 to 0.50 increases net profit.

Corrections to the audit

The backtest contradicts three claims in the earlier audit. The ceteris-paribus method used there — solving each gate with all other features at their training mean — was misleading, because the features co-vary: when h4Body is large, h4Slope is large too (same-sign weight) and the 2h/4h velocity features sit below their means (opposite-sign weight), so the h4Body needed in live data is far below the isolated figure.

Method, and what it cannot claim

This is an independent Python re-implementation of GVCalculate and the EA's execution layer — not MetaTrader's Strategy Tester, which needs MetaEditor. Four validation checks were run first: H4 rebuilt from the 10-year H1 series matches H4 aggregated from M5 to $0.000 on all 1,192 buckets complete in both feeds; M5→H1 aggregation matches the H1 file to $0.007 mean over 5,531 bars; no duplicates, OHLC violations or zero-range bars in any file; and a harness bug that silently disabled the H4 gate was caught by cross-checking the qualified-signal count two ways, then fixed before any result here was produced.

  1. No tick data. The intrabar ordering assumption is the largest source of uncertainty and it flips the sign. Closing this is the single highest-value next step.
  2. Constant spread. Real gold spreads widen at rollover and on news — exactly when shock is high and this EA still trades. A time-varying spread would hurt more than the flat sweep suggests.
  3. 12.4 months, one instrument, one regime — a strong gold bull market, with 380 trades for a per-trade edge of $0.80.
  4. Two feed holes — 22.7 days and 13.3 days, about 10% of the window. 11,740 M5 bars were excluded because their lookback spans a hole.
  5. EnableAutomaticEntries was forced true. As shipped it is false, so the EA out of the box takes no trades at all.

What to do next

  1. Get tick data for this window and re-run. Until the bracket collapses, no configuration decision is supportable. Nothing else changes what is knowable.
  2. Fix audit findings H1 and H3 first. The per-tick history scans make a multi-year MT4 tester run impractical, and the naked-stop path is a live blocker regardless.
  3. Re-derive the model, or drop it. The probability heads add nothing here. Either retrain on this broker's feed with a real out-of-sample split and a reliability diagram for P10, or strip the model and trade the H4-body condition directly — that is what the coefficients already do, and it would at least be testable.
  4. Do not tune on this sample. Several variants beat the default, but all sit inside the same noise band. Re-tuning here is curve-fitting 380 trades.
  5. If traded at all, trade it small and forward. The measured edge is +0.05 R per trade with no demonstrated stability across halves.
